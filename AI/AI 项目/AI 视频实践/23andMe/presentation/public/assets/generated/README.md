# Generated visual assets

These two raster masters were generated with the built-in image generation tool and then copied into the project. They are original project assets; neither one copies a source composition or motif.

## `one-sale-zine-background-v2.png`

- Role: chapter-opening wash and chapter-closing poster background
- Skill recipe: `gc-minimal-zine-poster-v0-1`, Standard Mode
- Format: PNG, 971 × 1619 px
- Visual colors: warm aged paper, near-black ink, one tomato-red anchor
- Recipe axes: lower-right small cluster; receipt + DNA thread + archive tab; typewriter + narrow serif typography; xerox/risograph/letterpress defects; flat scan
- Revision: v2 preserves the v1 composition while enlarging the misregistered tomato-red block to approximately 1.0–1.4% of the canvas so the color anchor remains visible when the image is used as a low-opacity background

Final prompt:

> Vertical 3:5 canvas on warm aged off-white uncoated paper, flat overhead scan with faint fold ghosts and uneven toner. Keep 78–85% of the sheet as quiet negative space. Place a single small subject cluster in the lower-right third, leaving the upper-left and center almost empty for web typography.
>
> The cluster is a tiny torn receipt backed by one irregular, slightly misregistered tomato-red payment block, joined by a thin DNA-like specimen thread to an oversized dark archive tab that disappears beyond the lower edge. The metaphor should read as one small transaction attached to a much longer obligation. Keep the cluster only 15–20% of the canvas. Use high-chroma tomato red only for the payment block and one short registration slash, about 1–2% of the whole canvas.
>
> Add sparse editorial typography: render exactly “ONE SALE” once in small distressed typewriter capitals near the receipt, and exactly “LIFETIME DUTY” once in narrow black serif capitals, partially cropped at the lower edge. Use black ink, subtle halftone, xerox grain, risograph misregistration, letterpress crush, torn-paper edges, and scanned-paper defects. No additional words, no random glyphs, no logos, no watermark.
>
> Quiet Japanese/Korean indie editorial-zine mood, handmade and unresolved, flat scan rather than a mockup. Avoid glossy commercial advertising, cinematic lighting, 3D, neon, gradients, polished vector graphics, dense scrapbook collage, large centered subject, decorative stickers, fake product photography, or clean digital typography.

## `lifetime-duty-pen-illustration-v1.png`

- Role: expressive illustration for the responsibility-imbalance beat
- Skill recipe: `antibes-holiday`, story-scene grammar + scale-distance composition
- Format: PNG, 1672 × 941 px
- Visual colors: warm-white paper, black fine-pen ink, one flat tomato-red coin
- Master type: high-resolution expressive raster master
- Reference role: the bundled stroke-calibration sheet controlled pen physicality only; its marks, shapes, positions, and composition were ignored

Final prompt:

> Create one original sparse black-pen story scene on plain warm-white uncoated paper. A tiny shallow payment pan at the far left holds one small flat tomato-red coin. Across a long imperfect balance beam, the far-right side is pulled down by an oversized open archive drawer. A loose DNA-like specimen filament emerges from a small sample tube, crosses the quiet gap as the causal path, and bunches into one compact dark knot where it enters the archive drawer. The viewer should infer: one small payment happened once, but sensitive genetic material must be carried and stored for a very long time. No captions are needed.
>
> Use a scale-distance composition with exactly one main proportional violation: the archive drawer is much too large and crops into the right edge; the tiny coin remains remote. Let one dominant structural sweep form both the sagging beam and the filament route. Keep the densest recognition knot off-center at the drawer opening. Let blank paper carry the long duration and imbalance. The payment pan, sample tube, beam joint, drawer opening, and filament should remain readable but incomplete.
>
> Draw as if in one alert sitting with a real fine black pen after one drink too many: one broad contour drift that changes the silhouette, one visibly missed or overrun junction, one displaced restart, one feature assembled from two fragments that disagree, one darker local retrace at the archive entry, and one to four purposeful false starts. Uneven pressure, slight ink pooling, dry breaks, open contours, blunt terminals, subtle paper tooth. Use black ink plus only the single flat tomato-red coin; no other color.
>
> No people, faces, hands, arrows, motion icons, labels, text, logos, watermark, decorative distress, uniform wobble, crosshatching, clean vector minimalism, polished editorial elegance, professional sketch anatomy, symmetric tableau, centered triangle, equal-size objects, perspective grid, gradients, shadows, scenic background, or fully closed product outlines. Stop when action, mass, and the recognition knot are inferable.
